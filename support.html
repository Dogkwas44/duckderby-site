Duck Derby — Support

Need help with Duck Derby? Email us at stinkysstudio@gmail.com and include your device model, the game edition (iPhone, Android or PC) and what happened. We usually reply within a few days.

Common questions

  • My ducks are too tired to race. Each duck has 4 energy and recovers 1 every 30 minutes, even while the game is closed. Practice mode on tracks you have won never uses energy. On mobile, a Duck Treat refills one duck to full.
  • Restore purchases. Open Shop > Extras > Restore to get back "Remove Ads" on a new device signed in to the same Apple ID / Google account. Duck Treats are consumable.
  • Ad choices. Change personalised ads under Settings > Privacy / ad choices, or in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
  • Progress lost? Saves live only on your device; there is no cloud account. Uninstalling deletes your progress.
  • Delete my data. Uninstall the game. We do not hold any personal data about you — see the Privacy Policy.
