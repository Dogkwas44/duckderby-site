Duck Derby — Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 31, 2026

Duck Derby ("the game") is made by Stinky's Studio ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the game uses, why, and the choices you have. It applies to both editions of the game:

  • Mobile edition (iPhone / Android) - free to play, with ads and optional in-app purchases.
  • PC edition - no ads, no tracking, no in-app purchases.

The short version

  • We do not have accounts, logins or servers. We do not collect your name, email, contacts, location, photos, microphone or camera.
  • Your game progress is saved only on your device.
  • On the mobile edition, our advertising partners (Unity LevelPlay and Google AdMob) receive a device advertising identifier and basic device information so they can show and measure ads. Ads are **personalised only if you agree**.
  • Purchases are handled entirely by Apple or Google. We never see your card or payment details.
  • The PC edition sends nothing: no ads, no analytics, no tracking.

1. Information stored on your device

The game saves your progress (your ducks, their names, stats and bond, coins, items, barn upgrades, unlocked races, trophies, settings, your ad-consent choice and whether you bought "Remove Ads" or Duck Treats) in a file on your device. This data stays on your device. We cannot see it and it is not uploaded to us. Your operating system may include it in your own device backups (for example iCloud or Google device backup) according to your device settings.

2. Advertising (mobile edition only)

The mobile edition shows ads (banners and occasional skippable full-screen ads) to keep the game free. Ads are served through Unity LevelPlay (mediation by Unity Technologies) and Google AdMob (Google LLC), and possibly other ad networks that LevelPlay works with. To show, cap, measure and protect against fraud, these partners may collect:

  • your device's advertising identifier (Apple IDFA - only if you allow tracking - or IDFV; Google Advertising ID on Android);
  • coarse device and network information - device model, operating system version, language, screen size, carrier, approximate (country / city-level) location derived from your IP address;
  • ad interaction data - which ads were shown, taps, and crash / performance diagnostics of the ad SDK.

Personalised ads only with consent.

  • iPhone: the game asks through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", your IDFA is not shared and you get non-personalised ads. You can change this any time in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
  • EU, EEA, UK and Switzerland: you are shown a consent screen before personalised ads are used. If you decline, only non-personalised (contextual) ads are shown.
  • Everywhere: you can change your choice in the game under Settings > Privacy / ad choices. On Android you can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings.

Ad content is limited to a general / teen rating; gambling, dating and alcohol ads are blocked.

These partners process data under their own privacy policies:

  • Unity: https://unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy
  • Google: https://policies.google.com/privacy and https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

Remove Ads: buying "Remove Ads" turns off all banner and full-screen ads in the game.

3. In-app purchases (mobile edition)

Purchases ("Remove Ads", "Duck Treats" packs) are processed by the Apple App Store or Google Play. They handle your payment details; we never receive your card number or billing information. The game only receives a confirmation that a purchase was made (a receipt) so it can unlock the item and restore it later.

4. Notifications (optional)

If the game offers reminders (for example "your ducks are rested") and you allow notifications, these are local notifications scheduled on your device by the game itself. No push token or personal data is sent to us. You can turn them off at any time in your device settings.

5. What we do not do

  • No accounts, sign-in, chat or user-generated content shared with other players.
  • We do not sell your personal information, and we do not collect personal data ourselves.
  • No analytics SDK of our own. (Ad partners may collect diagnostics as described in section 2.)
  • The game does not use the camera, microphone, contacts, photos or precise location.

6. Children

Duck Derby is rated for teens 13 and up and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided information through the game, contact us at stinkysstudio@gmail.com and we will help remove it (see section 7).

7. Your choices and data deletion

  • Delete your game data: uninstall the game. This removes the save file from your device (also clear any device backup that contains it if you want it gone there too). Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing on our side to delete.
  • Advertising data: change your choice in Settings > Privacy / ad choices, in iOS Tracking settings, or by resetting your Android advertising ID. To ask an ad partner about data it holds, use the links in section 2.
  • Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR / UK GDPR or US state laws such as the CCPA), you may have rights to access, delete or object to processing of personal data. Contact us and we will help, including passing the request to our ad partners where relevant.

8. PC edition

The PC edition has no ads, no in-app purchases, no analytics and no tracking. It saves your progress only on your computer and does not send any information over the internet. (If you get the PC edition through a store such as Steam, that store's own privacy policy covers your store account.)

9. Security

Your save file stays on your device. Communication between the ad / store SDKs and their servers uses encrypted connections (HTTPS) provided by those SDKs and your operating system.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy, for example when the game adds a feature or changes an ad partner. The new version will be posted at this address with a new effective date. Significant changes will also be noted in the game's update notes.
